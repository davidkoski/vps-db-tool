            First of all, I would thank jpsalas, akiles50000, dark and destruk to allow me reskin Jolly Park pinball.
            This table works with 10.7.2, I added musics, new sounds during the game and reskin the table.

            - The flippers buttons activate the magnets
            Magnets can move the ball in upper left of the playfield.

            - Rightmagnasave button = next track

            - The rom is jolypark.zip

            - Extract OFest.rar then put OFest folder in music folder.
